## Intent

Round-2 relaunch video for Plinks, "the living instrument". The redo turned a static physics toy into a responsive musical object: typing rains letter-beads through the pegs, the board strums under the cursor, the marble drops with sparks and camera shake, and the settled score is a scrubbable instrument. The video demos those five moves in order — type, strum, drop, scrub, share — on real captured footage, ending on the URL. Silent: kinetic type in the product's fonts carries it, readable with sound off. Voice: quiet, workshop-precise, no hype.

## Assets

- Real product footage: `assets/session-1080.mp4` — a 33.9s directed Playwright session at 1920x1080 on the production build: idle, typing beads, peg strumming, plunger drop, score scrub, share ticket.
- Product identity: bone paper #F5EFE6, walnut #2B1F14, brass #C9A227, ember #E8503A; Fraunces display italic, IBM Plex Mono labels.

## Customizations

- The five beats mirror the in-app first-run guide's teaching order.
- End card: play-plinks.vercel.app over the settled board, walnut ground.

## Notes

- Silent by design: `music: none`, no narration, no sfx. Type and motion do the work.
- The product's own motion language is the reference: letter beads raining, pitch-colored sparks, the ticket rising like a printed card.
